Puzzle still untouched?

Click Junior start or Senior start (whichever difficulty you play) and then Solve. The pegs load at the game's original positions.

Already moved the pegs? (most likely)

The original layout can't be restored in the game, so rebuild your current board here. Your screenshot never leaves your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. In the game, drag the pegs apart so every line between them is easy to read, and take a screenshot.
  2. Click Load… (or paste with Ctrl+V) and load your screenshot. A crop tool opens — drag the box over just the playable area (the pegs and lines), not the wooden frame border around them, then Use crop. Including the frame border throws off where the solver thinks the pegs are. Use Recrop… later to redo it without reloading. (If you clicked Junior start or Senior start earlier, click Clear first to start from an empty board.)
  3. Add peg: click each peg on the screenshot.
  4. Connect: click two pegs to add a line between them, and repeat for every line. Click the same two again to undo. Use Delete line / Delete peg for mistakes, and Lines to make lines translucent so you can see the screenshot.
  5. Open Debug / export / import below the board and check the counts: Junior has 15 pegs and 30 lines, Senior has 18 pegs and 43 lines.
  6. Click Solve. Every peg that has to move turns green; pegs that stay grey don't move. For each green peg, find it at its dashed circle (where it is now) and drag it, in the game, to the green peg at the end of the arrow (where it should go). Overwhelmed, or pegs overlap? Click Step by step at the bottom left of the board: it shows one move at a time, with the peg to move ringed in yellow and a green circle marking where to drop it. Use ▶ for the next move once you've made that move in the game. Click Stop while searching to use the best result found so far.

Peg numbers are just the order you added them, so they don't match anything in the game.
